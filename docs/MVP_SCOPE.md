# TimeDrop MVP Scope

## Problem

Recipients often cannot receive parcels during unpredictable delivery attempts.
The pilot explores whether authorized parcel intake, temporary custody, and recipient-selected delivery windows can improve first-attempt delivery success.

## Initial user roles

1. Customer: views an eligible parcel and selects an available delivery slot.
2. Operations admin: registers an eligible parcel, records custody, and schedules delivery.
3. Delivery partner: views assigned deliveries and records delivery outcomes.

## MVP workflow

1. Operations staff registers a parcel that TimeDrop is authorized to handle.
2. The system creates a unique tracking reference.
3. The customer receives a secure notification/link (later milestone).
4. The customer selects an available date and time window.
5. Operations assigns the parcel to a delivery partner.
6. The delivery partner records delivered or failed, with an outcome reason.
7. The customer can view the status timeline.

## Explicitly out of scope for the first milestone

- Direct integration with Amazon, Flipkart, or other marketplaces.
- Taking custody of parcels without written authorization.
- Payments, automated refunds, or COD collection.
- Nationwide logistics coverage.
- AI route optimization before we have reliable operational data.

## Pilot success metrics

- First-attempt delivery success rate.
- Percentage of customers selecting a delivery slot.
- On-time delivery rate against the selected window.
- Cost per successful delivery.
- Parcel loss/damage incidents.
- Customer and delivery-partner satisfaction.
