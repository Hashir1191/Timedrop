# TimeDrop Brand & Document System

**Status:** Initial brand direction for product and document implementation  
**Brand name:** TimeDrop  
**Descriptor:** Smart Parcel Delivery  
**Proposed tagline:** Reliable Deliveries. On Time, Every Time.

> This is a product-brand specification, not a statement that a legal company name, registered office, contact number, or domain has been established. Do not print invented legal or contact details on customer documents.

## 1. Brand positioning

TimeDrop is an India-first last-mile delivery scheduling and coordination platform. Its brand should communicate reliability, transparency, secure handling, and clear delivery coordination.

**Personality:** dependable, modern, precise, approachable, operationally disciplined.

**Writing style:** concise, calm, respectful, and action-oriented. Avoid exaggerated delivery guarantees. Use precise statuses and timestamps.

## 2. Visual identity

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary navy | `#0B2E6B` | Wordmark, document headers, footer |
| Accent blue | `#167BFF` | Links, highlights, progress and key actions |
| Pale blue | `#E8F2FF` | Section backgrounds and table headers |
| Text | `#172B4D` | Main document text |
| Muted | `#64748B` | Secondary labels and metadata |
| Success | `#16A34A` | Delivered / completed |
| Warning | `#F59E0B` | Attention / delayed |
| Error | `#DC2626` | Failed / exception |

Use white as the primary page background. Use colour sparingly so documents remain legible when printed in grayscale. Status must always include text, not colour alone.

## 3. Logo usage

The primary logo is the TimeDrop wordmark paired with a parcel-and-motion symbol. The symbol represents a parcel moving through a reliable, time-aware delivery network.

- Use the full wordmark on website headers, receipts, reports, and partner material.
- Use the symbol alone only where space is limited, such as a favicon or compact app icon.
- Preserve clear space around the logo; do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or recolour it arbitrarily.
- Use the navy/blue logo on white or pale backgrounds. Use a white variant on dark navy only after a dedicated inverse asset is supplied.
- Do not add a registered-trademark symbol unless the mark is actually registered.

## 4. Document system

All printable documents share one visual system, while each template contains only information appropriate to its purpose.

### Shared document header
- TimeDrop logo and descriptor.
- Document title and document category.
- Document/reference number.
- Generated timestamp and timezone.
- Optional department or facility label, only when known.

### Shared document body
- Clear section headings with pale-blue accents.
- Consistent label/value pairs and readable tables.
- Tracking number and parcel identifier where relevant.
- Status labels with both colour and text.
- Page numbers for multi-page reports.
- Avoid splitting a row or signature block across pages.

### Shared footer
- “Generated by TimeDrop” and the generation timestamp.
- Verification instructions and a QR code only when a real verification URL/code is available.
- Company legal name, registered office, phone, email, and website must come from approved configuration. If not configured, omit these fields rather than inventing them.

### Document templates
1. **Parcel registration receipt:** confirms system registration only; it must clearly state that it is not proof of physical custody or final delivery.
2. **Delivery receipt / proof of delivery:** includes actual delivery status, recorded recipient, timestamp, and available proof references. Never fabricate a signature or delivery event.
3. **Warehouse intake report:** includes parcel identifiers, received condition, intake operator, facility, and recorded custody event.
4. **Partner handover acknowledgement:** includes the actual custody event, partner identity, signed name, captured signature, and timestamp.
5. **Finance receipt / transaction report:** includes only recorded payment/transaction data; do not imply payment completion unless the transaction status confirms it.
6. **Support case report:** includes case reference, category, status, priority, relevant public conversation, and resolution details. Exclude internal-only notes and private attachments from customer-facing copies.
7. **Operations / management report:** includes report period, generated timestamp, filters, summary metrics, detailed tables, and operational notes. Every metric must be calculated from the selected dataset and period.

## 5. Basic instructions printed on documents

Instructions must be specific to the document type, not generic legal claims.

- **Registration receipt:** “Keep this receipt for your records. Registration confirms that the parcel details were recorded in TimeDrop; it does not confirm physical receipt or delivery.”
- **Delivery receipt:** “Check the parcel and delivery details. Report a discrepancy through the official TimeDrop support channel using the tracking number.”
- **Warehouse intake:** “Record visible package condition at intake. Escalate damage, mismatch, or missing identifiers before accepting custody.”
- **Partner handover:** “Verify the tracking number and package condition before signing. Do not sign on behalf of another person.”
- **Support case:** “Quote the case number in follow-up communication. Do not include passwords, OTPs, or full payment-card details.”
- **Operations report:** “Review the report period, filters, and generation timestamp before using these figures for operational decisions.”

Support contacts and response-time commitments must be configured and approved before printing. Do not print sample email addresses, phone numbers, or service-level promises.

## 6. Access, privacy, and document integrity

- A consistent design does not mean every employee can access every record. Existing authorization rules remain authoritative.
- Generate each document from the requesting user's permitted dataset and role scope.
- Apply data minimisation: show only fields required for the document's purpose.
- Mask personal contact details unless the document's purpose and access policy require the full value.
- Never expose internal support notes, private evidence, OTPs, credentials, or secrets in downloadable reports.
- Keep document number, issue timestamp, issuer, and content hash where verification is supported.
- QR codes must resolve to a real HTTPS verification endpoint; do not print decorative or placeholder QR codes as if they verify a document.

## 7. Print and accessibility standards

- Default paper size: A4 portrait; use landscape only for wide operational tables.
- Minimum body text: 9 pt for dense reports; prefer 10–11 pt for receipts and instructions.
- Use adequate margins and high contrast. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.
- Repeat table headers on subsequent pages and keep totals with their relevant table.
- Include report filters and timezone so figures can be interpreted later.
- Ensure important identifiers are selectable text in the PDF, not embedded only in images.
- Test on-screen rendering and physical grayscale printing before release.

## 8. Configuration still required before production printing

The following must be supplied and verified by the business owner:
- Legal entity name and registration details, if applicable.
- Approved registered/correspondence address.
- Official support phone and email.
- Official website/domain and document verification URL.
- Approved customer support instructions and response-time commitments.
- Approved logo artwork and any trademark clearance.

Until then, use the product brand “TimeDrop” and omit unverified company details.
